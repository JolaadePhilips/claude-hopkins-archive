# Claude Hopkins Archive

A visual, source-backed internet swipe file of advertisements and campaigns attributed to Claude C. Hopkins.

## What each ad record includes

- the original ad scan as the main object
- brand and date where known
- headline / product
- context for what the ad is doing
- why the execution is useful to study
- hook, offer / CTA and proof mechanism
- Hopkins principles used
- original publication when verified
- attribution and date confidence
- exact scan provenance
- source and verification links

## Editorial standard

Every gallery record carries its scan/image source and, wherever available, a separate verification source for authorship, campaign or date. We do not turn OCR guesses into facts. Bulk archive records retain their exact volume/page provenance even before they receive manual annotation.

## Archive backbone

The bulk ingest uses the public-domain Claude Hopkins collections made available through the CopyLegends / Matt Bockenstette vault. Each rendered scan preserves the volume and page it came from. Campaign-level verification sources include Hopkins' own *My Life in Advertising*, Library of Congress records, Smithsonian material, historical advertising research and other specialist archives.

## Public-domain / source handling

The historical advertisements date from the public-domain era in the United States. Modern scan hosts and archive websites are credited on each record. Source provenance is stored separately from the historical work itself.
